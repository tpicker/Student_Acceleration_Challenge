STUDENT EXPLORER / COURSE-ONLY SOURCE NOTES

This is a duplicate of the approved global explorer with country and U.S. state
settings for course students. It introduces no outside data or empirical location
assumptions. The original AI power model and energy calculation are retained.

INPUTS RETAINED
AI readiness categories and defaults: low 1-10 MW (default 5), medium 50-200 MW
(default 100), high 500 MW-1+ GW (default 750). The original course readiness model
is embedded verbatim, including input validation. Course AI horizon ranges remain
5-10, 10-15, and 15-20 years. Custom MW outside the category range are identified.
The horizon is not a construction schedule and is not shortened to four years.

Technology capacity, investment ($M), and timing ranges: the same 13 technologies
from the course technology energy calculator. Multiple units use independent,
additive ranges as an illustration, not a factual scaling relationship. No shared
cost or scale effects are assumed. Dollar ranges are not annual operating costs.
AI investment is not added because its scope could overlap energy investment.

Student Acceleration Challenge: the 16 countries, seven states, January 2027
start, December 31, 2030 target, hypothetical 2027 SMR milestone, and advice choices.
The milestone remains a course story premise, not a factual event or a guarantee.
It does not override the course SMR range of 4-7 years.

Country context: the existing country roadmap's ten factors and the existing
country stakeholder app's nine roles. State context: the existing state roadmap's
ten factors and state stakeholder app's 17 roles. Original source labels are kept.
Country and state risk activities remain course tools; this app adds no risk score.
The matching context links switch with the setting. State links retain addresses
the user supplied in this course conversation, including the original course risk
analysis address. No new publication address is assumed for a renamed state app.

FORMULAS
AI MW = sum(AI demand-unit count x MW/unit), using the original model's precision.
The 2027-2030 course comparison assumes a full four-year window is available.
Generation counts by default only if its entire course timing range is <=4 years.
If the user enables earliest timing, options whose range starts within four years
but extends beyond it also count. Options whose range starts later remain excluded.
This is a coarse reference comparison, not a delivery or feasibility forecast.

Lower nameplate gap = max(0, AI MW - upper counted generation MW).
Upper nameplate gap = max(0, AI MW - lower counted generation MW).
Existing state/country supply and existing demand are not represented. A zero gap
does not establish dependable supply, grid headroom, or overall AI readiness.
Storage MW and unsplit Solar + Storage MW remain separate from generation.
No storage duration, energy equivalence, or generation/storage split is invented.
Hydropower retains its open-ended upper capacity marker.

Energy investment = sum(unit count x lower endpoint) to sum(unit count x upper
endpoint) for the whole selected mix, including storage, hybrids, and later options.
The optional MW x (365 x 24 hours) calculation is a full-load reference conversion
to MWh, not an annual-use prediction or a supplied utilization assumption.

NOT PROVIDED
The inputs do not supply location electricity totals with matching years, capacity
factors, utilization, electricity prices, operational constraints, or environmental
rates. No actual annual electricity use, annual generation, electricity share,
annual electricity cost, dependable supply gap, emissions, water totals, jobs, GDP,
ROI, payback, or economic benefit forecast is added. Missing inputs are not zero.

PROVENANCE
course-reference-data.json contains the input snapshot, source-file hashes, course
app links, and hashes of the global files preserved when making this duplicate.
All reference values, decision factors, and roles come from the course apps.
