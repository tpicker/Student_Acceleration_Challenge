PUBLISHING

CHANGES
- Both proposed and revised MW totals are editable at the bottom of the page.
  Enter a saved MW amount directly (including formatted values such as 1,250 MW),
  or use the total calculated from the AI Readiness Combination of Levels.
  Editing an AI combination returns that side to its calculated MW total.
- The comparison shows proposed AI demand and revised AI demand, separately from
  generation capacity selected in each energy portfolio.
- Removed the marked MW notice, instruction paragraph, and AI Energy Lab header
  and footer branding. Applied the requested comparison labels and placement.
- Replaced the confusing schedule/cost text-entry fields with calculated energy
  cost and schedule estimates. Old text records are preserved in copied/printed
  summaries when an earlier saved draft contains them.
- Solar is a separate choice from Battery Storage; Solar + Storage is removed.
- Tidal Basin and Oil/Petroleum are available with three blank per-unit assessment
  inputs: MW, cost (million USD), and schedule (years). The original source table
  contained no presets for these technologies. No numerical range was invented.
  Missing values mark the corresponding total incomplete and show a known subtotal.
- Small Modular Reactor remains in the catalog with its unchanged 4–7 year range.
  Known options outside four years remain visible but disabled in the 2030 picker.
  Custom options with no schedule can be selected for assessment; their timing is
  unconfirmed until entered. If an entered schedule exceeds four years, it is
  explicitly flagged. An already selected option is never silently deleted.
- AI counts and MW on both sides are editable as planning targets. Course AI
  planning horizons are reported separately; energy timing does not establish
  that AI readiness will be completed by 2030.

COPY / PRINT / SAVE
Copy Summary remains at the top and bottom, with a read-only manual-copy fallback.
Printing includes proposed/revised MW, quantities, energy estimates, timing, and
the selected target-credibility judgment. No responses are submitted automatically.
Each location retains separate browser-local entries; the Student version also
separates state/country. New version-3 storage reads old version-2 or version-1
drafts without overwriting the old keys. Stable technology IDs prevent shifted
ratings after Solar + Storage is removed. Old combined entries are identified
for review; no invented split between Solar and Battery Storage is applied.

MODEL / APPEARANCE
The original ReadinessModel, all twelve retained technologies' numerical ranges,
location lists, story cards, target date, original styles and colors are retained.
Costs remain indicative quantity-based scenarios; schedules use the longest
selected range assuming overlapping work. Batteries are excluded from generation.
